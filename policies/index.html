Privacy Policy

Draft — requires review by Canadian legal counsel before launch

What we collect: the information you give us to schedule and perform service — name, phone, email, service address, vehicle details, and notes you add to a request.

Why we collect it: to confirm your appointment, perform the service, send your photo report and invoice, and honour the 45-Day Ride Promise. We do not sell personal information.

How we contact you: by text or email about your request and appointment. Marketing messages only with your explicit consent, with a working unsubscribe.

Retention & access: service records are kept for warranty and tax purposes. You may request a copy or deletion of your personal information at hello@rollandcharge.com, subject to records we must keep by law (PIPEDA applies).

Terms of Service

Draft — requires review by Canadian legal counsel before launch

Appointment requests are not confirmed until Roll & Charge responds with a confirmation. Prices published on this site are for standard passenger vehicles; duallies, commercial, and specialty fitments are quoted individually. HST applies to all prices.

You confirm you own or are authorized to authorize work on the vehicle, and that we have safe, legal access to it at the service address. We may decline or stop work where conditions are unsafe.

Our liability for any claim relating to a service is limited to the amount paid for that service, except where law provides otherwise.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Draft — requires review by Canadian legal counsel before launch

Rebook or cancel free up to 24 hours before your window — reply to your confirmation text or email us. Inside 24 hours we may apply a $49 rebooking fee. No-shows (no access to the vehicle at the address given) may be charged the same fee. (Fee amount to be confirmed by owner before launch.)

Weather Delay Policy

We work in most weather — rain and normal snow included. In severe conditions (ice storms, lightning, extreme cold warnings) we will text you to rebook into the next available slot at no charge. You never lose a fee to weather.

Service Warranty

Draft — requires review by Canadian legal counsel before launch

Workmanship on every job is covered by the 45-Day Ride Promise below. New tires carry their manufacturer's warranty; we'll help you register and claim it. Flat repairs are warranted for the legal life of the repair — if a properly repaired puncture fails, we'll re-repair or credit the repair fee toward replacement.

45-Day Ride Promise — Terms

Draft — requires review by Canadian legal counsel before launch

If anything tied to our work feels off within 45 days of service — a vibration after balancing, a slow leak on a repaired tire, a TPMS light we reset — we return and make it right at no charge. Covers our workmanship; does not cover new damage (impacts, punctures, curb strikes) after the visit. Claims: text or email us with your invoice number.

Tire Storage Terms

Draft — requires review by Canadian legal counsel before launch

Stored sets are washed, photographed, tagged, and kept in dry, insured storage. Storage runs per season ($119) with redelivery at your next swap. Sets unclaimed 12 months after the final paid season may be disposed of after two written notices. (Insurance details and disposal window to be confirmed.)

Payment & Refund Policy

Draft — requires review by Canadian legal counsel before launch

Pay on completion — card, tap, or e-transfer. No deposits for standard residential service. If you're not satisfied, tell us before the technician leaves and we'll fix it on the spot or adjust the invoice; after that, the 45-Day Ride Promise applies. Refunds for services not delivered are issued in full to the original payment method.

Accessibility Statement

We want this site usable by everyone. It's built to WCAG 2.1 AA targets: keyboard navigable, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, labelled forms, and AA colour contrast. Found a barrier? Email hello@rollandcharge.com and we'll fix it and accommodate your booking by phone or text in the meantime.
