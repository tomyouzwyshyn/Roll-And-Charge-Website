Storage Questions

Why store with you instead of my garage?

Garages are fine if tires stay cool, dark, dry, and away from a furnace or EV charger's ozone. If yours means stacking in a warm corner — or just means clutter — $119 buys back the space and slows the aging.

Is the Swap & Store bundle actually cheaper?

Yes: $239 vs $258 booked separately ($139 swap + $119 storage) — and it's one visit, not two arrangements.

What if I sell the car mid-season?

Text us — we'll arrange redelivery or transfer to the new owner's address inside Halton at no extra charge.

Are stored tires insured?

Yes, stored sets are covered while in our care, and the intake photos document condition. Full terms on the policies page.
