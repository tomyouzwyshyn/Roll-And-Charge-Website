The Program.

Simple enough to start with two vans.

Scheduled Seasonal Swaps

We come to your yard on a date you pick — before the first snow call, not after — and run the whole line: torque to spec, pressures logged, photos per vehicle.

Rapid Flat Response

A van down is a route down. Fleet flats jump the queue during business hours, with published pricing so your dispatcher isn't negotiating from a shoulder.

One Invoice, Real Records

Consolidated monthly billing, per-vehicle service history, tread readings at every visit — the paper trail your insurer and your ops manager both want.

Priced Like Everything Else We Do: Openly.

Fleet work starts from the same published rates as every driveway in Halton — $139 on-rim swaps, $229 changeovers, $119 flat repairs — with multi-vehicle, same-visit pricing that rewards density (you've seen our $79 second-vehicle rate; imagine ten in a row at your yard). Volume programs are quoted per fleet, in writing.

Storage for the off-season sets, new tires and rims supplied at fleet pricing, and EV fleet vehicles handled with the same EV-safe procedures — no surcharge.

Fleet Questions.

What size fleet do you take?

From 2 vehicles to around 50. Light-duty focus: vans, pickups, SUVs, cars, and light commercial. Medium/heavy trucks and equipment are quoted case-by-case.

Can you work outside our operating hours?

That's the point — evenings, weekends, and split visits so your fleet never sits during billable hours. We schedule around your dispatch, not ours.

Do you store fleet winter sets?

Yes — picked up at the swap, stored insured, redelivered in the fall. Storage is $119/season per set, with fleet-volume pricing in the written quote.

How do we start?

Email hello@rollandcharge.com with your vehicle count and home yard. You'll get a written program quote — rates, response commitments, billing — usually within two business days.
