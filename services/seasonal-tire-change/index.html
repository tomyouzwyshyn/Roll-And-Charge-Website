Seasonal Change Questions

When should I book my seasonal change?

Follow the 7°C rule: when daytime highs stay below 7°C (late October in Halton), winters go on. Book mid-October to beat the first-snow rush — the winter tire guide has the full timing breakdown.

On-rim or off-rim — which am I?

If your winters live on their own rims, you need the $139 swap. If both seasons share one set of rims, tires must be dismounted and remounted — the $229 changeover with balancing included. Not sure? Send a photo of your spare set with the request.

Do I need to be there?

No — leave the tires accessible (garage code, side gate, tagged in the driveway) and we handle the rest, with photos texted when done.

Does a mobile seasonal change qualify for the winter tire insurance discount?

Yes. Ontario insurers must offer a discount for four installed winter tires; our invoice is your proof, same as any shop receipt.
