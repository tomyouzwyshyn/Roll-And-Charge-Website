Flat Repair Questions

Which punctures can be repaired?

Tread-area punctures up to about 6mm (a screw or nail) repair reliably with an internal patch. Sidewall damage, shoulder punctures, long cuts, or a tire driven flat are replacement territory — and we'll say so before charging you anything beyond the visit.

Is a plug-and-patch permanent?

Done from the inside with the combination method — yes, it's a permanent repair endorsed by tire manufacturers, unlike outside-in rope plugs which are get-you-home fixes.

What if I have no spare?

Most EVs and many new cars don't. If the tire is repairable, we fix it on the spot. If it isn't, we quote a replacement delivered and installed — often same-day.

Can you come to a highway shoulder?

Where it's safe, yes; where it isn't (live 400-series lanes), we'll get your spare on fast or meet you at the nearest exit or lot — safety calls the play. Full details on the roadside page.
