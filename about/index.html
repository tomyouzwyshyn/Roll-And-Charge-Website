Roll & Charge exists because of a simple observation: nothing about changing tires requires you to spend your Saturday in a waiting room. So we put the whole tire bay in a van and brought it to Halton.

What We Believe.

Your time is the product. A tire swap takes under an hour of work — the industry just makes you donate half a day around it. Booking a 2-hour window at your own driveway gives that day back.

Prices belong in public. Every price we charge is published on this site before you book — $139 swaps, $229 changeovers, $119 flat repairs. If an insured mobile competitor beats us in writing, we match them. Mystery invoices are a choice, and we choose not to.

Proof beats promises. We're new to Halton, so we don't ask for faith: every lug torqued with a calibrated wrench to your manufacturer's spec, photos of finished work texted to you, a free re-torque check after 50 km, and the 45-Day Ride Promise on everything we touch.

The Standard on Every Job.

Torque You Can Verify

Calibrated torque wrench, manufacturer spec, every lug — and a free re-torque after 50 km. Impact-gun-and-hope is how wheels come loose.

45-Day Ride Promise

Vibration, slow leak on a repair, TPMS light — anything tied to our work in 45 days and we come back free. In writing, on every invoice.

EV-Native From Day One

The "Charge" is not decoration: lift-point pucks, EV torque specs, acoustic tire sourcing. Your EV costs the same as any car here.

Where We Work.

All of Halton Region — Oakville, Burlington, Milton and Halton Hills — at homes, offices, job sites, GO lots, and the occasional highway shoulder. Mon–Fri 8am–6pm, weekends 9am–5pm.

Judge Us by the First Visit.

Book a swap, watch the process, keep the photos. That's the pitch.
