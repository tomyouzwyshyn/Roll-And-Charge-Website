Burlington Questions.

Do you cover all of Burlington?

Yes — Aldershot, Millcroft, Alton Village, The Orchard, Brant Hills, Roseland, Tyandaga, Headon Forest and everywhere in between, plus offices along the QEW, 403 and North Service Road corridors.

Can you do my swap while I'm at work or on the GO train?

That's the point. Park at home or your office lot, leave the tires accessible, and we'll do the swap while you're at your desk or riding in from Burlington, Appleby or Aldershot GO. Photos by text when done.

How fast can you get to me in Burlington?

During changeover season we run daily Burlington routes with same-day slots when available. Flat repairs are prioritized — tell us where you are and we'll text a real ETA.

How much does a mobile tire change cost in Burlington?

$139+HST for a seasonal swap on rims, $229+HST for a full changeover off rims (mount, balance, TPMS reset). Second vehicle in the same driveway: $79 on-rim, $159 off-rim. Every price is on our pricing table.

When should Burlington drivers switch to winters?
