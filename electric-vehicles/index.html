What EV-Safe Actually Means.

Not a sticker — a different procedure at four specific points.

Lift-Point Pucks

EV battery packs run edge-to-edge under the floor. We lift only on the manufacturer's designated jacking points using protective pucks — never on the pack or its skirt.

EV Torque Specs

EV wheels carry more mass and more instant torque. Every lug is torqued to your manufacturer's EV-specific spec with a calibrated wrench, with a free re-torque check after 50 km.

TPMS + Pressures

Correct placard pressures matter double on an EV — range and wear both punish underinflation. We set pressures cold and reset TPMS where supported.

Why EV Tires Wear Faster — and What to Do About It.

Extra battery weight plus instant torque means EV tires can wear 20–30% faster than the same tire on a gas car. The fixes are boring and effective: correct pressures, on-schedule rotations, and the right tire — XL-load-rated, ideally the OE acoustic (foam-lined) version that keeps the cabin quiet and the range honest.

We source and install those exact tires — quoted all-in, with a written-quote price match.

EV Questions.

Do you really charge the same price for EVs?

Yes. EV-safe handling is our standard procedure, not an upsell — $139 seasonal swap, $229 changeover, same as any car in Halton.

Can you work in my garage next to the charger?

Usually yes — the kit is fully portable and height-restricted garages are fine. Note the clearance and which side your charge port is on when you book.

Do I need acoustic (foam-lined) tires?

If your EV came with them, replacements should match — the foam liner cuts cabin drone noticeably at highway speed. If it didn't, they're a nice upgrade but not mandatory. We'll quote both ways.

Can a punctured EV tire be repaired?

Often yes — same rules as any tire: tread-area punctures under 6mm repair well with a proper internal plug-and-patch; sidewall damage means replacement. Foam-lined tires take a little longer (we peel back and re-seat the liner) but repair fine.

Which EVs do you service?

All of them — Tesla Model 3/Y/S/X, Polestar 2, Ioniq 5/6, Mustang Mach-E, EV6, bZ4X, Leaf, Bolt, and every PHEV. If it has wheels and a battery, we have the specs.
