Halton Hills Questions.

Do you cover all of Halton Hills?

Yes — Georgetown and Acton plus Glen Williams, Stewarttown, Limehouse, Norval, Ballinafad and Silver Creek, and the rural roads between them. Long or gravel laneway? Just note it when you book.

How much does a mobile tire change cost in Halton Hills?

$139+HST for a seasonal swap on rims, $229+HST for a changeover off rims (mount, balance, TPMS reset). Second vehicle at the same address: $79 on-rim, $159 off-rim. Every price is on our pricing table.

Can you do my swap while I'm at work or on the GO train?

That's the point — Georgetown GO and Acton GO commuters leave the car, keep the tires accessible, and come home to the job done. Photos by text.

Do you service rural properties and hobby farms?

Yes — our kit is fully self-contained, so a barn-side spot outside Ballinafad works like a Georgetown driveway. Trailer and light-truck tires quoted individually.

When should Halton Hills drivers switch to winters?

Below 7°C daytime highs — and up here on the escarpment side that comes earlier than by the lake. Late October is the smart window. Our Ontario winter tire guide →

How fast can you get to me?

Georgetown and Acton routes run several days a week in season, with same-day slots when available. Flats are prioritized — we'll text a real ETA.
