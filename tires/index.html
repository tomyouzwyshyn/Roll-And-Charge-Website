How Tire Buying Should Work.

No showroom, no waiting room, no guessing what the out-the-door number will be.

1. Tell Us Your Size

Your tire size (sidewall or door jamb) or just year/make/model — plus how you drive. Highway commuter, escarpment winters, EV: it all changes the right answer.

2. Get Real Options

We come back fast with good/better/best options and one all-in number each — tires, delivery, installation, balancing, taxes. No "plus fees" surprises at the door.

3. Installed at Your Door

Pick your option and a 2-hour window. We mount, balance, torque to spec, set pressures, reset TPMS, and take your worn tires for recycling.

Tire & Rim Questions.

How fast do quotes come back?

Fast during business hours — usually the same day. Tell us your size or vehicle and how you drive, and we return good/better/best options with one all-in number each.

Will you install tires I bought somewhere else?

Yes — that's our Tire Changeover at $229+HST for four tires, off the rims: mount, balance, TPMS reset, and disposal of the worn set included.

Which brands can you get?

All the majors — Michelin, Bridgestone, Continental, Yokohama, BFGoodrich, Pirelli, Goodyear, Toyo — plus value lines when budget is the priority. We'll tell you honestly when the mid-tier tire is the smart buy.

Do you do EV and acoustic tires?

Yes — XL-load-rated and OE acoustic (foam-lined) tires for Tesla, Polestar, Ioniq, Mach-E and other EVs, installed with EV-safe equipment.

What happens to my old tires?

We take them — recycling is included in every installation, handled through Ontario's tire recycling program.
