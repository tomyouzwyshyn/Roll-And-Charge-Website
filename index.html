FAQ

Questions, Answered.

How much does a mobile tire change cost in Oakville, Burlington or Milton?

A seasonal swap on rims is $139+HST for most vehicles; a full changeover off rims (mount, balance, TPMS reset) is $229+HST. A second vehicle in the same driveway is just $79. Everything is published in the pricing table — no hidden trip fees inside our core Halton area.

Do I need to be there while you work?

No. As long as we can reach your vehicle and tires (driveway, garage code, concierge), you can be anywhere. We text before arrival and send photos when we're done.

What's included in a seasonal tire swap?

All four wheels off and on, torque to manufacturer spec with a calibrated wrench, pressures set to your door placard, tread and sidewall inspection, and a free re-torque check. TPMS reset included where supported.

Can you work in condo or underground parking?

Usually yes — our kit is fully portable and height-restricted garages are fine. Just add the clearance and parking level in your booking notes.

What if it rains or snows?

We work in most weather. In severe conditions (ice storms, lightning) we'll text to rebook you into the next available slot free of charge.

How long does it take?

A swap on rims typically takes 45–60 minutes; a changeover off rims runs 60–90 minutes depending on the vehicle.

Run-flats, low-profile, or 20″+ wheels?

Yes — they carry a small +$10/tire surcharge (it's in the pricing table) because they take longer to mount safely.

Are you safe for EVs — Tesla, Polestar, Ioniq?

Yes — it's in our name. Lift-point pucks protect the battery skirt, we torque to EV-specific specs, reset TPMS, and can source OE acoustic tires built for EV weight and quiet cabins. More on EV tire service.

What if something feels off after the appointment?

That's the 45-Day Ride Promise: any vibration, slow leak on a repaired tire, or TPMS light tied to our work within 45 days — we come back and make it right, free.

Do you sell new tires and rims too?

Yes — all major brands plus winter wheel & rim packages, delivered and installed at your door. Found a lower written quote from an insured mobile installer? We'll match it. Start a tire quote.
